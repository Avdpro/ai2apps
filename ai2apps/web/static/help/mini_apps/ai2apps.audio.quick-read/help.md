# Quick Read

Paste or type up to 10,000 characters, choose an installed speech model and one of its actors, then select **Generate and play**. No project or script segments are needed.

If a speech model is not ready, use **Configure speech** first. Actors come from the selected model; models without named actors use their default voice. Changing models clears the actor selection.

Text and voice choices are saved in the Mini-App draft. Generation status, playback and download appear in the shared **Preview & Output** panel; choose WAV, MP3, M4A (AAC), or FLAC beside Download audio, then choose a location in Save As. The latest 20 generated results are restored after refreshing or restarting the app, including playback and download. Older results and their unreferenced audio files are automatically removed. Audio already added to Gallery remains available in Gallery. If automatic playback is blocked by the browser, press Play in the audio controls.

For long-form narration, multiple segments or a cast of characters, use Audio Book or Ensemble Drama.
